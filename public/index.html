How to play

Controls

  • WASDor arrow keys to move
  • Left clickPick up / put down / serve (or P)
  • Right clickHold about 2 s to chop (or hold O)
  • TAim with the mouse, left click to throw (T again or Esc cancels)
  • SpaceSprint: 1.6x speed for 5 s, then 10 s to recharge

Hearts and orders

  • Both chefs start with the same number of hearts (5 to 8, picked in the waiting room).
  • Ingredients pop up on the floor and vanish after 8-10 s. Walk over one and left click (or press P).
  • Chop on your board, cook in your pot, plate it, and serve at your counter (stations in your colour).
  • Serve an order before its bar runs out to clear it and get a speed boost (1.3x for 3 s). If the bar runs out, you lose the hearts shown on the order.
  • A finished pot burns if you leave it for 10 s.
  • Hit your rival with a thrown ingredient: they're stunned for 2 s and drop what they carry.

How to win

  • Last chef standing: lose your last heart and you're knocked out. Your rival wins straight away.
  • Time up: regular time is 3 minutes. Then the chef with more hearts wins (half hearts count, so 4½ beats 4).
  • Sudden Death Dish: if hearts are level, both chefs get the same random dish. The first to serve it wins. Nobody serves it within 1 minute? It's a draw.
  • Both chefs lose their last heart at the same moment? It's a draw.
