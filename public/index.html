How to play

Controls

  • WASDor arrow keys to move
  • Left clickPEPick up / put down / serve
  • Right clickROHold about 2 s to chop
  • TQStart aiming at the mouse pointer
  • Left clickWhile aiming: throw what you hold (T / Q again or Esc cancels)
  • SpaceShiftSprint: 1.6x speed for 5 s, then 5 s to recharge
  • 1234Emotes: 👍 😂 😡 🔥 pop up above your chef
  • CheatThe Cheat button above the kitchen: turns cheat mode on, click again to turn it off
  • WSWSADADSecret combination: also turns cheat mode on (arrow keys work too)

Cheat mode

  • Lasts 15 s, then needs 15 s to recharge. Both chefs see a CHEAT badge while it's on.
  • Chopping and cooking are instant.
  • Every dish you serve takes that dish's hearts off your rival.
  • Every throw that hits your rival takes ½ heart.

Hearts and orders

  • Both chefs start with the same number of hearts (5 to 8, picked in the waiting room).
  • Ingredients pop up on the floor and vanish after 8-10 s. Walk over one and left click (or press P / E).
  • Chop on your board, cook in your pot, plate it, and serve at your counter (stations in your colour).
  • Serve an order before its bar runs out to clear it and get a speed boost (1.3x for 3 s). If the bar runs out, you lose the hearts shown on the order.
  • Rush orders: serve an order while more than half of its bar is left and your rival gets an extra order on top of their own. Cook fast to bury them in orders!
  • Streak: every 3 dishes in a row without a missed order gives you ½ heart back (never more than you started with).
  • Orders speed up every minute: new orders arrive more often and their bars get shorter.
  • A finished pot rings, then beeps faster and faster, and burns if you leave it for 10 s.
  • Hit your rival with a thrown ingredient: they're stunned for 2 s and drop what they carry (then they can't be stunned again for 1 s).
  • Throw an ingredient at your own empty chopping board or your pot and it lands right on it.
  • Steal: raid your rival's chopping board (take what's on it) or plate up their finished pot with an empty plate. Their plates, serving counter and hands are off limits.
  • The label under your chef says what a click will do right now.

How to win

  • Last chef standing: lose your last heart and you're knocked out. Your rival wins straight away.
  • Time up: regular time is 3 minutes. Then the chef with more hearts wins (half hearts count, so 4½ beats 4).
  • Sudden Death Dish: if hearts are level, the kitchen is cleared and both chefs get the same random dish. The first to serve it wins. Nobody serves it within 1 minute? It's a draw.
  • Both chefs lose their last heart at the same moment? It's a draw.
  • A chef who leaves in the middle of a round loses it: the chef who stayed wins.
