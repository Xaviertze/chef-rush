How to play

Controls

  • WASDor arrow keys to move
  • PPick up / put down / serve
  • OHold about 2 s to chop
  • TAim with the mouse, press T again to throw
  • CCheat mode on / off
  • EscCancel aiming

How to score

  • Ingredients pop up on the floor and vanish after 8-10 s. Walk over one and press P.
  • Chop on your board, cook in your pot, plate it, and serve at your counter (stations in your colour).
  • A served order scores its points plus a speed tip. Late orders vanish and score nothing.
  • Hit your rival with a thrown ingredient: they're stunned for 2 s and drop what they carry.

How a round ends

Each round lasts 2½ minutes. The chef with more points wins; a tie is broken by dishes served.
