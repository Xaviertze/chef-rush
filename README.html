Chef Wars: Showdown Battle

A real-time 1v1 cooking survival game for two browsers (COMP4021 group project).

1. Run it

npm install
node server.js

Then open http://localhost:8000.

Each player needs their own browser session, because the sign-in is a cookie. Use a normal window plus an incognito window, or two different browsers. Register a different account in each one.

Accounts and results are saved in data/users.json, which is created automatically.

2. Start a game

  1. Register (or sign in) in both windows.
  2. Click Find match in both windows. The two players are paired and taken to the waiting room.
  3. Optionally pick the starting hearts (5 to 8), then both press I'm ready!. The game starts after a 3-2-1 countdown.

3. How to play

ControlAction
WASD or arrow keysMove
Left click, P or EPick up the ingredient you stand on, or pick up / put down / serve at the station you face
Hold right click, R or OChop on your chopping board (about 2 seconds)
T or Q, then left clickAim with the mouse and throw the ingredient you hold
SpaceSprint for 5 seconds (then 5 seconds to recharge)
1 2 3 4Emotes
  • Ingredients pop up on the floor and vanish after 8 to 10 seconds. Both chefs race for them.
  • Stations framed in your colour are yours (red on the left, blue on the right). The label under your chef says what a click will do.
  • Garden Salad: chop lettuce and tomato, put both on a plate. Tomato Soup: two whole tomatoes in the pot. Veggie Stew: chop carrot and onion, cook them in the pot. Put pot dishes on a plate, then serve at your serving counter.
  • Every order you miss costs the hearts shown on its card. Lose your last heart and you're knocked out.
  • Serve an order with more than half its bar left and your rival gets an extra rush order. Three dishes in a row give half a heart back.
  • Hit your rival with a thrown ingredient to stun them for 2 seconds.
  • Regular time is 3 minutes; the chef with more hearts wins. If hearts are level, a Sudden Death Dish (up to 1 minute) decides it. A game therefore lasts at most 4 minutes.

4. Cheat mode

  • On: click the Cheat button above the kitchen (or type W S W S A D A D).
  • Off: click the Cheat button again. It also turns off by itself after 15 seconds, then needs 15 seconds to recharge.
  • While it's on: chopping and cooking are instant, and every dish you serve takes that dish's hearts off your rival, so you can knock them out quickly.

5. Shorter rounds for testing (optional)

The round length can be changed with an environment variable, for example a 60-second round:

set GAME_TIME=60 && node server.js      (Windows cmd)
GAME_TIME=60 node server.js            (macOS / Linux)
